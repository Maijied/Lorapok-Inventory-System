# Entering a shop

## What this is

Signing in as one of a shop's own staff, to see what they see. It is the most
dangerous thing in the system: acting inside somebody else's business.

## When it is appropriate

- A shop reports something you cannot reproduce from the outside.
- You need to see a screen as a specific role to answer their question.

## When it is not

- Curiosity about a shop's numbers.
- Doing something for them that they could do themselves. Teaching them
  where the button is lasts longer than pressing it for them.
- Anything you would not want to explain to that shop afterwards. You may
  have to: they can ask what was done and why.

## What it records

The reason you type is stored and shown to the shop in a banner for the whole
session. Every write you make is logged. A rejected or expired link is logged
too — that is the signal one leaked.

## Read-only by default

You get read-only unless you explicitly ask for write access. Ask for it only
when you are going to change something, and say so in the reason.

## The link

Sixty seconds, single use. If it expires, mint another — do not keep one
around. A link that still works tomorrow is a standing key to somebody's shop.
