# Overview

The first screen after signing in, and a summary of today.

## The tiles

What you see depends on your role. A cashier sees takings and what needs
reordering; margin and stock value are separate permissions, because cost is
commercially sensitive.

**Today's takings** is revenue — sales minus refunds — not the raw total that
went through the till.

**Needs reordering** counts products at or below their reorder level. If it
reads zero and you know you are short of something, that product has no
reorder level set.

## Running low

The five products closest to running out, worst first. It is the list to act
on before a customer asks for something you cannot sell them.
