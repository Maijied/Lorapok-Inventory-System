# Reports

## Takings and revenue are not the same

**Takings** is what came into the till. **Revenue** is takings minus refunds.
Reports show revenue, because that is what the shop actually kept.

## Margin

Revenue minus what those items cost you, using the cost recorded at the moment
each one sold. This is the number that says whether a busy month was a good
month.

If margin looks impossibly high, a product is missing its cost price — the
system treats the whole selling price as profit. Reports flags nothing here;
check **Products**.

## Stock value

What the stock on your shelves cost you, not what it would sell for. That is
the figure that matters for cash flow: it is how much of your money is sitting
in the shop rather than in the bank.

## Who can see this

Margin and cost are separate permissions from the rest of reports. A cashier
can be given reports without being given margin.
