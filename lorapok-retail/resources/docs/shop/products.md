# Products

## What a product is here

Anything you sell. A handset, a cable, a screen repair. The difference that
matters is whether it is **stock-tracked** and whether it is **serialised**.

- **Standard** — counted, not individually identified. Cables, cases.
- **Serialised** — every unit has an IMEI or serial. Handsets.
- **Service** — no stock at all. A repair, a screen protector fitting.

Marking something serialised when it is not will make you type a serial for
every cable you sell. Marking a handset standard loses the ability to trace a
specific unit.

## Cost price

Fill it in. It is what makes profit knowable — the system records what each
item cost you at the moment it sold, so buying cheaper next month does not
change what last month earned. Left empty, the product sells fine and its
margin reads as the whole selling price, which is a lie you will act on.

## Reorder level

The quantity at which the shop should reorder. It drives **Running low** on
the overview. Set it to what a week of selling looks like, not to zero — a
reorder level of zero tells you only after you have already run out.

## Removing a product

Products are hidden, not deleted. Its sales history stays, because deleting it
would change what last quarter earned.
