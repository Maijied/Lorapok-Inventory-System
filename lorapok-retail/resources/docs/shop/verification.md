# Verification

## Why we ask

We need to know who your shop is legally before we can invoice you. It is the
same information you would give a bank or a supplier opening an account.

**You can keep selling while this is outstanding.** Verification gates
billing, not the till.

## What we need

- The business name **as it appears on your trade licence**, which is often
  not the name above the door
- The trade licence number
- The owner's full name as it appears on their NID
- The owner's NID or passport number
- A photograph of the trade licence and of the NID

BIN or TIN, the back of the NID, and a photograph of the owner are optional.

## What happens to it

Your NID and TIN are **encrypted** before they are stored. Anyone reading the
database directly — including us — sees ciphertext.

The photographs are not on the public internet. Reaching one requires a link
that lasts five minutes and only works for a signed-in reviewer, and **every
time one is opened is recorded**: who, and when.

If we reject your application you can ask us to delete the documents. The
record that a decision was made stays; the photographs do not.

## Filling it in

Save as you go. A half-finished form is kept, so you can come back to it —
a trade licence number is not something anyone enjoys typing twice.

Once you send it, it locks while we read it. That is so a document cannot
change halfway through a review.

## If it comes back rejected

You will see why at the top of this page, specifically enough to fix. Correct
it and send it again; the old rejection clears when you do.
