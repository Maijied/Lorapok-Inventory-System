# Your logo

The icon your staff see once they install the till on a phone or tablet.

Until you upload one, Lorapok generates an icon from your shop's first letter
in your shop colour. That is a real icon — nothing is broken if you never
change it. Uploading your own just makes the till look like your shop rather
than like our software.

## What to upload

- **PNG, JPG or WEBP.** Up to 4MB.
- **Roughly square works best.** A wide wordmark still works — we centre it
  rather than stretching it — but it ends up small inside the square.
- **SVG is not accepted.** An SVG file can carry code, and this picture ends up
  on someone's home screen. A 512&times;512 PNG looks identical at icon size.

We resize whatever you send to 512&times;512 and leave a margin around it.
Android crops icons into a circle, a squircle or a rounded square depending on
the phone, so anything right up against the edge would get cut off.

## Changing or removing it

Upload a new file and it replaces the old one. **Remove** puts the generated
icon back.

A phone that has already installed the till may keep showing the old icon for
a while — that is the phone's cache, not us. Reinstalling the till updates it
immediately.

## Who can do this

Anyone with the **manage settings** permission. Cashiers cannot change it.
